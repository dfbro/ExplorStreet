# ExplorStreet — deterministic road-following sample routes

Date: 2026-10-10
Status: implemented and visually checked on Android-sized React Native Web.
Android native build: **not executed**.

## Why the old routes looked square

The earlier `route-sketch.ts` constructed one of three small polygons from
fixed latitude/longitude offsets, selected by a character-code hash of the
route name. Leaflet connected those offsets as straight segments; they were
not based on any street data. It also used flat oval markers.

The old polygons and obsolete SVG `path` / `start` fields were removed.

## New geometry source

Six route snapshots are now generated by **FOSSGIS OSRM** from the OpenStreetMap
road graph, using the dedicated foot or bicycle profile. Results are
deterministically saved in
`src/features/map/road-route-data.ts`.

| Route ID | Mode | Road-graph distance | Simplified positions |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| ijen-run | foot | 3.67 km | 73 |
| kayu-run | foot | 3.38 km | 82 |
| sukun-run | foot | 7.17 km | 121 |
| lowok-bike | bike | 9.68 km | 100 |
| blimbing-bike | bike | 8.71 km | 89 |
| ijen-bike | bike | 12.90 km | 169 |

Generation: `python3 scripts/generate-road-samples.py`.
This command contacts the public routing service and must only be used
occasionally for a noncommercial prototype; it enforces at least 1.2 seconds
between requests. The routing engine version/map graph can change over time.
The **snapshot** is served by the app itself and makes **zero requests to
FOSSGIS during normal runtime**.

The source endpoints have the profile in the host path:
`https://routing.openstreetmap.de/routed-foot/route/v1/driving/...`
and `.../routed-bike/route/v1/driving/...`. The trailing `driving`
path segment is required by that deployment's OSRM API, while the
**routed-foot/routed-bike** instance selects the actual profile. Avoid
substituting the car-only `router.project-osrm.org` demonstration service.

Every requested anchor snapped within 77 m of a road segment; output routes
returned valid GeoJSON line strings. Geometry was simplified only within
2.25 meters to keep the snapshot small while retaining bends and junctions.
Model distances (not straight-line estimates) come from OSRM and are used by
the route catalog. Durations and ascent remain estimates.

## Map UI

- The **green solid line** is model-generated on the OSM road network.
- The **Material Icons `place` pin** is used for start, end/destination,
  and cafes, with colors distinguishing each type. Loop start/end show a
  single green pin, not an oval.
- **Gambar konsep** remains a separate manual-drawing mode with an amber
  dashed straight-line sketch and an explicit "not road-snapped" notice.
- **Reset** restores the saved road-derived geometry.
- Unknown route IDs show no fabricated random fallback geometry.

## Limits and next steps

**Routing-graph alignment is not on-the-ground validation.** OSRM follows
ways permitted by its configured foot/bicycle profile, but conditions,
traffic, construction, sidewalk availability, access restrictions, barriers
and personal safety have not been independently checked. The sample lines
are not endorsed as safe physical routes and are not live navigation.
Do not advertise measured ascent, actual pace or confirmed amenities.

For production, commission or deploy a routing/POI service with a usage SLA,
and field-check or community-review the planned routes before promoting them.
